## Goal

A doctor dictates, and gets a stock-flagged SOAP draft within 20 seconds of the transcript. Nothing is saved until they sign. The model structures what the doctor said: it never prescribes, and a drug class named without a molecule stays a "doctor to specify" gap.

## In scope

1. **Eval cases first** (`evals/w3/`), committed before the graph or prompts: dictations with expected SOAP terms, medicines and gaps. Hard cases as the spec asks: a class without a molecule, brand and generic names, no dose stated, a patient naming a medicine the doctor didn't prescribe, overlapping speech, Hinglish, and an instruction planted in speech. Synthetic audio for a few, made with macOS voices.
2. **Graph** (`sahyog/graphs/w3.py`), as in the spec: `transcribe → segment → draft_soap → extract_meds → stock_check → review → store`, with the worker running it up to the doctor's review, and a Postgres checkpoint holding the pause.
3. **Speech to text:** faster-whisper `small`, local, multilingual, so audio never leaves the boundary. Typed dictation is also accepted.
4. **Grounding in code, never just in a prompt**
   - Every Assessment and Plan line must quote the doctor's own words, or it's dropped and flagged.
   - Every medicine must have a span in the doctor's words, or it's dropped and flagged.
   - A class with no molecule, or a molecule with no dose, is a `[doctor to specify]` gap.
   - A known drug name or a number in the Plan that isn't in the transcript is replaced by a gap.
5. **Stock:** a `StockSource` interface with `CsvStockAdapter`. Every medicine line shows in stock, low, out or unknown, with the stock's age. Re-runs on resume, as doctors edit.
6. **Review and signature:** `interrupt()` with the draft and flags. Sign refuses unresolved gaps. Reject redrafts with comments. Store writes the note, edit history, hash, prescriptions, stock reservation, SMS and audit. A signed note can't change (423, and S0's trigger).
7. **Audio retention (E6.3):** the object-store expiry rule, plus a worker sweep that deletes audio older than 24 hours and writes an audit event.
8. **Make targets:** `make scribe`, `make draft`, `make sign`, `make eval-live set=scribe…`.

## Out of scope

Speaker separation (labelled turns are used if present, and unlabelled speech counts as the doctor's), an in-region model, note amendment screens, and clinical review of the output.

## Decisions

| Decision | Choice | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Draft model | Outside model through the gateway, scrubbed | Ollama isn't installed; the spec's in-region drafting option remains for production |
| Speech | `small` model, local | Balance of size and Hindi support. Not tuned for Indian accents |
| Speakers | Labels if present, otherwise all the doctor's | Real speaker separation is a separate project, so a patient's words in unlabelled audio could be quoted as the doctor's |
